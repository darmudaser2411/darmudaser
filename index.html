About

I received PhD in power engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Guwahati. My doctoral studies were carried out under the supervision of Dr. Sanjib Ganguly, Associate Professor, Department of Electronics & Electrical Engineering. My doctoral thesis, “Voltage Control of Active Distribution Networks with EV Charging Stations Using Multi-Time Scale Model Predictive Control,” develops centralized and distributed control strategies for voltage regulation and energy loss minimization in modern distribution networks facing high penetration of photovoltaic generation and electric vehicle charging loads.

My work sits at the intersection of power systems, optimization, and control theory — with a particular focus on model predictive control (MPC) and chance-constrained formulations for managing uncertainty from renewables and EVs, using minimal measuring and communication infrastructure.

I hold an M.Tech in Electric Power and Energy Systems from the National Institute of Technology Srinagar and a B.Tech in Electrical Engineering from the Islamic University of Science and Technology, Jammu & Kashmir.

Research Projects (PhD)

Centralized Voltage Control with Minimal Infrastructure

Developed a model predictive control based centralized voltage control strategy with energy loss minimization in highly penetrated distribution networks, requiring minimum measuring and communication infrastructure.

Voltage Control Under Renewable & EV Uncertainty

Developed a chance-constrained model predictive control based centralized control strategy to contain nodal power uncertainties in highly penetrated active distribution networks.

Distributed Coordinated Control Strategies

Developed a model predictive control based distributed coordinated voltage control strategy for active distribution networks.

Distributed Control Under Uncertainty

Developed a chance-constrained model predictive control based distributed coordinated voltage control mechanism to account for nodal power uncertainties in highly penetrated active distribution networks.
